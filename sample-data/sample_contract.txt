SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between:
ABC Corporation, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 123 Business Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("Service Provider")
and XYZ Company LLC, a limited liability company with its principal place of business at 456 Client Avenue, New York, New York 10001 ("Client")

1. SERVICES
Service Provider shall provide software development and consulting services to Client as described in Exhibit A attached hereto (the "Services").

2. TERM AND TERMINATION
2.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier as provided herein. The Agreement shall expire on December 31, 2028.
2.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other party in the event of a material breach of this Agreement by the other party that remains uncured after thirty (30) days written notice of such breach.
2.3 Termination for Convenience. Client may terminate this Agreement at any time upon ninety (90) days prior written notice to Service Provider.
2.4 Notice Period. All notices required under this Agreement shall be provided in writing and shall be deemed effective upon receipt. For purposes of termination notices, the notice period shall be calculated from the date of receipt of the notice.

3. RENEWAL
3.1 Automatic Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.
3.2 Renewal Terms. The renewal terms shall be on the same terms and conditions as this Agreement, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT
4.1 Service Fees. Client shall pay Service Provider the fees set forth in Exhibit B (the "Service Fees"). Payment is due within thirty (30) days of receipt of invoice.
4.2 Late Payment. If Client fails to make any payment when due, Client shall pay interest on the overdue amount at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

5. OBLIGATIONS

5.1 Service Provider Obligations. Service Provider shall:
(a) Perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner;
(b) Deliver all deliverables specified in Exhibit A by the delivery dates specified therein;
(c) Provide monthly status reports to Client by the fifth (5th) business day of each month;
(d) Maintain appropriate insurance coverage as required by applicable law; and
(e) Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the performance of the Services.

5.2 Client Obligations. Client shall:
(a) Provide Service Provider with timely access to Client's premises and systems as reasonably required for the performance of the Services;
(b) Review and approve deliverables within fifteen (15) business days of receipt;
(c) Pay all invoices in accordance with Section 4.1; and
(d) Designate a single point of contact for all communications under this Agreement.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all confidential information disclosed by the other party. This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

7. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
7.1 Limitation of Liability. Service Provider's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
7.2 Indemnification. Service Provider shall indemnify Client against any third-party claims arising from Service Provider's breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

8. MISCELLANEOUS
8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
8.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.
8.3 Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both parties.
8.4 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except in connection with a merger or acquisition.
